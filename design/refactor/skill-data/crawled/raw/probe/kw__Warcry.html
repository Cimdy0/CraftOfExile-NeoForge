warcry GemTags /25
Infernal Cry
Warcry, AoE, Trigger, Fire, Duration, Nova
Perform a Warcry, Empowering subsequent Melee Attacks if there are enemies nearby. Enemies in the warcry's area are destabilised and will Combust on death. This Skill's cooldown can be bypassed by expending an Endurance Charge.
Ice Bite I
Support, Warcry, Cold, Duration
Supports damaging skills and Warcries you use yourself. Freezing an enemy with supported skills infuses your damage with Cold damage for a short time.
Ice Bite II
Support, Warcry, Cold, Duration
Supports damaging skills and Warcries you use yourself. Freezing an enemy with supported skills infuses your damage with Cold damage for a short time.
Bhatair's Vengeance
Support, Attack, Warcry, Cold, Duration
Supports Attacks and Warcries you use yourself. Freezing an enemy with Supported Skills infuses you and your allies with Cold damage for a short time.
Dropped by: Global
Skeletal Brute
Minion, Warcry, Persistent, Physical, Payoff
Activate to summon hard-hitting, Reviving Skeletal Brutes that can Stun Primed enemies and Warcry on Command. This Warcry Intimidates nearby enemies and Consumes Freeze on enemies and allies to deal damage in an Area around them.
Seismic Cry
Warcry, AoE, Physical, Nova
Perform a damaging Warcry, Knocking Back nearby enemies and Heavy Stunning enemies that are Primed for Stun. If an enemy is Heavy Stunned or a currently Heavy Stunned enemy is Hit, this Skill Empowers subsequent Slams to perform an additional Aftershock. This Skill's cooldown can be bypassed by expending an Endurance Charge.
Ferocious Roar
Warcry, Shapeshift, Bear, AoE, Nova, Meta
Shapeshift into a Bear and roar in defiance, immediately gaining Rage if there are enemies nearby and Empowering subsequent Attacks to Break Armour. Alternatively, socketing a human-form Warcry into this Skill triggers that Warcry instead, and augments its damage and area. This Skill's cooldown can be bypassed by expending an Endurance Charge.
Enraged Warcry I
Support, Warcry
Supports Warcries, causing them to spend Rage to bypass their usual Cooldown.
Enraged Warcry II
Support, Warcry
Supports Warcries, causing them to spend Rage to bypass their usual Cooldown.
Fortifying Cry
Warcry, Attack, AoE, Melee, Trigger, Physical, Duration, Nova
Perform a Warcry that grants Guard and Triggers Shield Wave when subsequent Shield Attacks deal damage. This Skill's cooldown can be bypassed by expending an Endurance Charge.
Raging Cry
Support, Warcry
Supports Warcry skills, causing them to grant you Rage on use depending on counted monster Power.
Armour Explosion
Support, Attack, Warcry, AoE, Trigger, Fire, Payoff
Supports Attacks and Warcries, causing them to trigger a Fiery explosion when they Fully Break an enemy's Armour.
Corrupting Cry I
Support, Warcry, Trigger, Physical, Duration
Supports Warcries, Triggering Corrupted Cry to inflict Corrupted Blood on enemies in their area of effect.
Corrupting Cry II
Support, Warcry, Trigger, Physical, Duration
Supports Warcries, Triggering Corrupted Cry to inflict Corrupted Blood on enemies in their area of effect.
Paquate's Pact
Support, Lineage, Warcry, Trigger, Physical, Duration
Supports Warcries, Triggering Twisted Pact to inflict Corrupted Blood on enemies in their area of effect., at the cost of your Life.
Dropped by: Maztli, Flesh-Shaper, The Bodach, Ytzara, Blood Oracle
Dazing Cry
Support, Warcry
Supports Warcries, causing them to Daze Enemies on use.
Battershout
Support, Warcry, AoE, Trigger, Physical, Payoff
Supports Warcry Skills. Supported Skills Consume Fully Broken Armour to trigger Physical Damage explosions on Enemies within their range but cannot themselves Break Armour.
Tireless
Support, Warcry
Supports Warcry Skills. Attacks Empowered by Supported Skills have a chance not to lower the Empowerment count of Supported Skills when they are used.
Lunar Blessing
Warcry, Attack, Shapeshift, Buff, Werewolf, AoE, Trigger, Cold, Duration
Shapeshift into a Werewolf and offer your Rage to the moon to receive a blessing, gaining bonus Cold damage for yourself and any Wolf Minions you have from Wolf Pack or Predator's Mark. Spends all Rage to extend the Buff's duration. While the Buff is active, your Werewolf Melee Attacks call down Moonbeams on Hit.
Arctic Howl
Warcry, Shapeshift, Buff, Werewolf, AoE, Cold, Duration, Nova
Shapeshift into a Werewolf and let out an icy howl that damages enemies and Freezes Primed enemies. If an enemy is Frozen or a Frozen enemy is Hit, this Skill Empowers your attacks with added Cold damage, causes Empowered Slams to create Chilled Ground, and grants your Allies added Cold damage. This Skill's cooldown can be bypassed by expending an Endurance Charge.
Ancestral Cry
Warcry, Attack, Buff, AoE, Melee, Slam, Projectile, Trigger, Fire, Duration, Nova, Conditional
Perform a Warcry using Glory gained by Igniting enemies to turn you into an embodiment of Kaom. While embodying Kaom, you Trigger Volcanic Steps while travelling, Melee Strikes and Slams are Ancestrally Boosted, and your Melee Strikes Trigger Volcanic Eruption.
Exposing Cry
Support, Warcry, Duration
Supports Warcries. Supported Skills apply Exposure on Hitting enemies.
Zerphi's Infamy
Support, Lineage, Warcry
Supports Attacks and Warcries you use yourself. Supported Skills gain a Glory requirement, and build Glory when you spend Life on Skills. Supported Skills deal more damage and have significantly increased area of effect.
Dropped by: Atziri, the Red Queen
Echoing Cry
Support, Warcry, AoE, Conditional
Supports Warcries. Supported Skills Echo twice, and their area grows larger each time, but are less effective and require you to have travelled a certain distance before use.
Vruun's Aftermath
Support, Lineage, Warcry
Supports Warcries, causing them to destroy Ice Crystals and Ice Fragments. Supported Skills empower additional Attacks if they destroy enough Ice Crystals and Ice Fragments.
